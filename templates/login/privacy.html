Spendwise Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Spendwise helps you record expenses, income, accounts, savings goals and budgets. This policy describes how the service handles information you provide when using the website or Android app.

Information we handle

We store your account name, email address, password hash, optional profile photo, and the financial entries and settings you choose to add. Imported files are processed to create transaction records. We also store service activity needed for security, page view counts, and notification subscriptions when you enable notifications.

How we use it

We use this information to provide your account, calculate reports and insights, send verification and password reset messages, deliver reports and reminders you enable, support the service, and protect accounts from misuse.

Service providers and external content

Hosting and email providers process data needed to operate Spendwise. If the optional AI motivation feature is enabled, saved and spent totals and a requested tone are sent to OpenAI to generate a short message; transaction descriptions and your email address are not included in that request. The app may fetch public jokes and market price information from external services. Web pages may load fonts or icons from external content providers. We do not sell your financial entries.

Storage and protection

Data is transmitted over HTTPS in production. Access to account data requires sign-in. We retain account data while your account is active, subject to operational backups and any retention required by law. We do not claim that internet transmission or storage is risk free.

Your choices and deletion

You can update profile details in Spendwise. To permanently delete your account and associated data, open Delete account, sign in, and confirm your account details. The link works from a web browser even if you have removed the app. Backup copies may persist until their normal rotation; any legally required retention will be explained if applicable.
